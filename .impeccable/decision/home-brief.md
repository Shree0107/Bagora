## Scope
Home page (`/`) of the VÉRANE store; the world extends to every storefront route (shop, product, story, cart, checkout, account, help pages). Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience and job
Women 25–40 in India, mostly on phones, often arriving from Instagram. They must quickly believe the bags are genuinely premium and reach a product. Primary action: open the collection / a bag. Proof: real photography, honest material and dimension facts, and real service terms (prepaid saving, COD, returns, GST invoice). No reviews or press exist yet, and none may be invented. Constraints: no "Paris" anywhere; pinned palette forest #1F3A2E, champagne #C9A96E, ivory #F5F1EB, espresso #3B2A20; Cormorant Garamond display and wordmark; refined sans body; subtle motion only.

## Direction contract
THESIS: VÉRANE's site is its own boutique front: a forest-painted maison façade with gilt lettering and one lit window. You stop at the window, read the sign and step inside. It refuses the category default of a full-bleed stock hero with a centred tagline over a card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Forest-painted joinery as the ground of the header, the window frame, the footer and full-width bands. Gilt champagne used as lettering, hairline mouldings and rules, never as fills behind text. The interior is ivory "shelving" (ivory grounds with deeper-ivory plinths) where the bags sit on 4:5 ivory frames. Cormorant capitals with wide tracking for signage, Jost for the shop-assistant voice. Buttons are glass-door plaques: a champagne hairline frame with uppercase tracked labels; the primary action is filled forest with gilt lettering. There are no rounded pills, no shadows, no gradients except the soft window light falling across the photo.

STORY: The visitor sees a real boutique window holding a real bag, understands at once that this is a leather house, and reads "Véritablement vôtre". Inside they browse by shelf (Tote, Crossbody, Shoulder, Work Bags), see the craft and material truth, and trust the service terms enough to buy on their phone.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile (390×844). A forest façade fills the screen. The sign band at the top has "VÉRANE" in gilt Cormorant capitals between two gilt mouldings; the menu and bag are set into the fascia corners. Below the sign, a deep window frame (a forest mullion border with an inner gilt bead) holds the hero photograph, about 60–65% of the viewport height, lit by soft window light. Lettered on the lower glass: the large italic "Véritablement vôtre" with a quiet subline beneath it ("Genuine leather handbags, crafted in India"), and the primary plaque [Discover the collection] sitting on the glass in reach of the thumb. A sill line with the four shelf names (Tote · Crossbody · Shoulder · Work Bags) acts as working links. On desktop the façade widens: the window becomes a wide display with the bag at the right third and the lettering on the left glass, and the shelf names run along the sill.

FORM: Rue Vitrine (Impeccable's pick, kind: pick, first on my ordered list) translated without Paris: the sign reads VÉRANE alone. seed: ce9c8f109789720e. Signature interaction: "stepping inside". On scroll past the window, the façade frame eases outward (scale and opacity) and the interior's ivory shelving rises into view, used once. Motion grammar: slow fades and a 1.04 zoom on bag frames when hovered, with reduced motion showing everything at rest.
